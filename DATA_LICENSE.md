# Data provenance and permitted research scope

Source: Binance public market data, https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data
and https://data.binance.vision/ . Data are attributed to Binance.

The current dataset terms (version 1, updated 2026-08-26) identify the dataset
license as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and permit private non-production research and
non-commercial educational use. The code repository's MIT notice does not
replace those dataset terms. Source terms:
https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md
and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ .

This project remains research-only. No commercial use, subscription, trading
execution or paid service is authorized by this data-foundation checkpoint.
Redistributed original files preserve provenance and source checksums;
normalization and research derivatives preserve attribution and applicable
share-alike requirements. Collection-time terms and revisions must be recorded
alongside future acquisitions; current terms do not prove the terms historically
in force for every source revision.

External providers have separate terms in `catalog/source_inventory.json`.
Discovery of an article URL does not grant rights to redistribute its full text.
Provider-restricted commercial data have not been purchased or downloaded.
